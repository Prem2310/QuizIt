============================================================
1. PRODUCT VISION
============================================================

QuizIt is a competitive aptitude and skill-improvement platform.

The core idea is:

"Practice alone, compete with friends, identify weaknesses, and continuously improve."

QuizIt combines:

1. Aptitude practice
2. Personalized practice
3. 1v1 real-time quiz battles
4. Friend challenges
5. Custom quizzes
6. Performance analytics
7. Weak-topic detection
8. Repeated practice of weak question types
9. Progress tracking
10. Gamification
11. Competitive rankings
12. Personalized insights

The application should feel like a modern competitive game rather than a traditional boring examination website.

The design inspiration is:

https://matiks.com/

Use Matiks only as UI/UX inspiration.

DO NOT copy its branding, assets, code, exact layouts, or proprietary content.

QuizIt's identity should be original.

============================================================
2. PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING
============================================================

Traditional aptitude websites usually provide:

- static question lists
- boring test interfaces
- little personalization
- limited analytics
- no meaningful competition
- weak motivation to continue practicing

QuizIt should solve this.

A user should be able to:

- practice aptitude topics
- discover their weak areas
- understand why they make mistakes
- repeatedly practice similar question types
- see measurable improvement
- challenge friends
- play real-time 1v1 quizzes
- create custom quizzes
- compare performance
- develop competitive motivation

The platform should answer:

"What should I practice next?"

instead of simply:

"Here are 100 questions."
